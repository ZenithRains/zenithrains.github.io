## Research question and approach

Diffusion weakens as either population density or nutrient concentration approaches zero. My advisor, Xiaoxin Zheng, and I seek an energy structure that handles both degeneracies and describes boundedness and long-term evolution on the plane.

The current manuscript combines chemical entropy, nutrient-gradient quantities, and mass compensation. Radius-uniform endpoint inequalities and moment iteration yield estimates, followed by compactness arguments for weak-solution construction. Under the stated initial-data and weighted gradient-energy assumptions, it studies globally bounded weak solutions, positive-time regularity, nutrient decay, and long-time population convergence.
