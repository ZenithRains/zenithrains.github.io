## Research question and approach

How does cusp attraction concentrate mass as diffusion weakens? My advisor, Xiaoxin Zheng, and I study a fractional aggregation equation, seeking to turn the balance between attraction and diffusion into a rigorous mass estimate.

We use a tent-shaped truncated moment to track local mass, decompose nonlocal boundary contributions, and combine radial geometry with Lorentz-space estimates. For nonnegative radial initial data and under explicit observation-time and scale conditions, the manuscript obtains a diffusivity-uniform positive lower bound for time-integrated mass at the intrinsic scale.
