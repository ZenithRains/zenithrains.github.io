I am Ruiyi Zhang, a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at Beihang University. I started my doctoral studies in 2024 and expect to graduate in 2029. I also completed my undergraduate studies at Beihang, in the Hua Loo-Keng Experimental Class for Mathematics.

My research focuses on nonlinear partial differential equations, particularly chemotaxis, aggregation-diffusion, and doubly degenerate diffusion models. I study how to choose suitable energy functionals, close chains of estimates, and use these estimates to understand properties of solutions. My joint manuscript with Xiaoxin Zheng on small-diffusivity concentration for a fractional aggregation equation has been submitted to *Mathematical Methods in the Applied Sciences*. A second manuscript on a planar doubly degenerate nutrient-taxis system is in preparation and has not yet been submitted. Titles, authors, and summaries are available under [Current research](/publications/).

I use ChatGPT and Codex to explore possible arguments, work through calculations, and organize material. I also examine the reliability of model-generated proofs, including missing assumptions, estimates used outside their valid range, and arguments with unresolved steps.

I hope to explore the role of large language models in real mathematical research, particularly open problems in analysis and partial differential equations. I plan to select problems relevant to my research from collections such as ICM Conjectures, check the original literature and the latest developments, break the problems down into testable intermediate propositions, and pursue them through model-assisted exploration and mathematical verification. Through this process, I hope to make meaningful mathematical progress and understand which stages of reasoning models can help with and where they remain prone to failure.

I am seeking internships in mathematical reasoning, model evaluation, and AI-assisted research in Beijing, with a planned start in October 2026. Please feel free to [email me](mailto:ruiyizhang@buaa.edu.cn) or view my [CV](/cv/).

Outside mathematics, I enjoy mountaineering and calligraphy, and I play the erhu. This website brings together my research, reading, travel writing, and ideas in progress. I use it to explain complicated questions clearly and to keep a record of views that I can revisit and revise.
