*Methodus inveniendi lineas curvas maximi minimive proprietate gaudentes*, subtitled *Sive solutio problematis isoperimetrici latissimo sensu accepti*, was published by Leonhard Euler in 1744 and is catalogued as E65.

Prepared by Ruiyi Zhang, this is a complete, AI-assisted Latin OCR transcription and newly typeset working draft. Its 212 pages contain six chapters, two appendices, 440 numbered textual units, and 49 vector figures. The figure plates are collected at the end of the book. The text uses EB Garamond and Garamond-Math.

Uncertain readings and editorial notes are retained in the draft. Transcription and interpretive errors may remain, and corrections are welcome. No Chinese translation is included. This working draft has not been independently established as a scholarly critical edition. Details of production, AI assistance, source materials, ornament attribution, and permissions appear in the PDF.

Source: [Euler Archive, E65](https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/65/). Title-page ornament reference: [ETH / e-rara copy](https://doi.org/10.3931/e-rara-1490).
